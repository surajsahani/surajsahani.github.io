1. Introduction

Welcome to SkyCars, an offline and peer-to-peer 3D futuristic flight simulation, racing, and aerial combat Android game developed by Suraj Sahani ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, use, and protection of information when you download, install, and play SkyCars from the Google Play Store.

Core Privacy Commitment: SkyCars is designed as a privacy-first game. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), accounts, contacts, location coordinates, photos, or browsing data to any external servers.

2. Information Collection and Device Hardware Usage

SkyCars runs primarily on-device. The game utilizes specific hardware APIs strictly for local gameplay mechanics as described below:

📱 Gyroscope & Motion Sensors (Rotation Vector / Accelerometer)

SkyCars offers optional tilt-to-fly steering. Pitch, yaw, and roll orientation values are processed strictly in real-time in the device memory to control the vehicle's flight physics. Sensor readings are never recorded, saved, or uploaded off the device.

💾 Local Storage & SharedPreferences

The game stores your garage customizations (body chasses, wing aerodynamic foils, pulse engine colors, booster thrust plumes, repulsor wheels, and paint hues), high scores, completed rings, and game settings strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via Android System Settings.

🌐 Optional Local Multiplayer Networking

If you choose to join a local multiplayer dogfight arena or race, the game utilizes local network/peer-to-peer connections (such as local Wi-Fi or hotspot) to broadcast ephemeral vehicle spatial transforms (X/Y/Z positions, velocities, orientation, health status, and weapon projectile firing). No personal credentials, device identifiers, or tracking IDs are shared.

🔊 Audio & Sound Synthesis

Audio is procedural and synthesized natively on-device using Android audio tracks. SkyCars does not request or use microphone or recording permissions.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

SkyCars does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral profilers, or invasive telemetry frameworks. We do not monetize through user data profiling.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR)

SkyCars is a family-friendly flight simulation game designed for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, account registrations, or communication logs, the game complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Data Security & Storage

Because all gameplay data (unlocked vehicles, customizations, mission progress) resides within your application's private Android sandbox, your data is protected by the operating system's standard security model. Uninstalling the application or using "Clear Storage" in your device settings immediately deletes all locally saved progress.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.

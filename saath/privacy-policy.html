1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to Saath ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). Saath is an Android mobile application developed and maintained by Suraj Sahani (operating under the developer moniker martialcoder).

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Saath software application on Android devices. It explains how personal and non-personal data is collected, processed, and protected when you interact with our services.

Core Commitment: Saath is built with privacy-by-design principles. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Saath, we may process certain minimal categories of data:

A. Information You Provide Directly

When you register, customize preferences, or contact our support team, you may provide your email address, name, or feedback messages.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information

To ensure reliable app performance and debug unexpected crashes, the app or standard Google Play frameworks may collect non-identifiable diagnostic metrics:

  • Device hardware model, manufacturer, and operating system version (e.g., Android 14).
  • Unique device identifiers, IP addresses (anonymized/truncated), and network connection type.
  • App performance metrics, crash stack traces, launch timestamps, and session durations.

3. Android Device Permissions

Saath only requests device permissions strictly required to provide specific application features. All sensitive permissions follow the Android runtime permission model and can be granted or revoked at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Saath > Permissions:

Permission Purpose Requirement
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Enables secure data synchronization with cloud services and network connectivity checks. Essential for connected features
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) Allows the app to deliver timely alerts, updates, and status messages to you. Optional / User Controlled
WAKE_LOCK Prevents device processor from sleeping during critical background synchronization tasks. System Level

4. How We Use Collected Information

We process information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Delivering Core Functionality: Providing the features, navigation, and tools offered within the Saath app.
  • Performance Optimization & Bug Fixing: Analyzing diagnostic logs to identify and resolve stability issues, freezes, and crashes.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our systems and users against malicious activities, unauthorized access, and abuse.
  • User Support: Responding to inquiries, feedback, and technical support requests submitted by users.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Requests

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance):

You have the right to request complete deletion of any associated account or personal data at any time. To initiate a data deletion request, email us at surajkumarsahani1997@gmail.com with the subject "Saath Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 business days.

7. Data Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse:

  • Encrypted Transmission: All data communicated between the application and servers is encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS protocols).
  • Secure Local Storage: Local app preferences and data caches utilize Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and sandbox isolation.
  • Restricted Access: Access to backend telemetry is restricted strictly to authorized development personnel on a need-to-know basis.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Saath does not address or knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal data without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records from our databases.

9. Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have specific statutory privacy rights under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to Restrict / Object: Object to or restrict specific forms of data processing.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your statutory privacy rights.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements, operational practices, or app features. When updates occur, the Effective Date at the top of this document will be revised. We encourage you to review this page regularly for any modifications.

1. Absolute Zero-Tolerance Commitment

Saath ("the Application"), operated by Suraj Sahani (martialcoder), is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful environment. We maintain a strict, non-negotiable Zero-Tolerance Policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), online grooming, or any endangerment of minors.

Immediate Action: Any user attempting to create, upload, solicit, distribute, or promote content involving the exploitation or abuse of children will face immediate permanent account termination, device banning, and direct reporting to national and international law enforcement agencies.

2. Prohibited Content & Behaviors

The following activities are categorically banned on Saath across profiles, bios, messages, media, and images:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual depiction, text, drawing, or media portraying minors in sexually explicit conduct or poses.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Grooming behaviors, solicitation of minors, sextortion, child trafficking, or trade of explicit content.
  • Facilitating Child Endangerment: Sharing links, contact info, or external groups dedicated to child exploitation.
  • Underage Participation: Creation of accounts or profiles by or on behalf of minors under 18 years of age.

3. Strict 18+ Age Restriction

Saath is an adult dating and relationship platform exclusively designed for individuals aged 18 years and older. Minors under the age of 18 are prohibited from using or creating accounts on the application.

If we discover that a user is under 18 or has misrepresented their age, their profile will be immediately banned and all associated data purged from active directories.

4. In-App and External Reporting Mechanisms

Saath provides multiple accessible reporting channels to allow users and observers to report child safety violations immediately:

A. In-App Reporting

Users can report any profile or chat message directly inside the app by tapping the Options menu (⋮) > "Report User" > "Child Safety / Underage Concern". Reports are flagged with the highest emergency priority for immediate moderation.

5. Reporting to Regional & International Authorities

In accordance with mandatory child safety laws (including US Title 18 U.S.C. § 2258A, Indian POCSO Act 2012, and IT Rules):

  • NCMEC Reporting: All confirmed instances of CSAM or child exploitation are immediately reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.
  • Indian Law Enforcement: Exploitation cases within India are forwarded directly to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and local police cyber cells.
  • Evidence Preservation: Pertinent server logs, IP addresses, device identifiers, and content hashes are securely preserved for law enforcement investigations.

6. Content Moderation & Enforcement

We deploy rigorous automated and human oversight measures:

  • Pre-Screening & Image Moderation: Profile photos and uploaded media undergo automated scanning to block explicit imagery and CSAM hash matches before becoming publicly visible.
  • Rapid Review: Safety reports involving child endangerment are treated as P0 emergencies and reviewed within hours.
  • Permanent Ban: Offending accounts, associated phone numbers, and device fingerprints are permanently blacklisted across our infrastructure.

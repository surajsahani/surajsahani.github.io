WebArt Privacy Policy

Interactive Spider Web Wallpapers & Art for Android

Effective Date October 30, 2026
Package ID com.martial.webart
Developer Suraj Sahani (martialcoder)
Zero Data Collection Guarantee: WebArt operates 100% offline on your device. We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information or usage data.

1. Overview

Welcome to WebArt ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Suraj Sahani (surajsahani.github.io). WebArt is an interactive spider web art studio and Android Live Wallpaper application designed for calming animations, creative web weaving, and minimal screen customization.

2. Information Collection and Use

WebArt does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or device data:

  • No Account or Registration: You can use all features of WebArt without signing up or providing any personal details.
  • No Personal Data: We never access or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or files.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ads: The application contains zero advertising SDKs, tracking scripts, or third-party analytics services.

3. Local Device Preferences

To remember your artistic customizations between sessions and apply them to your Live Wallpaper, WebArt stores your settings locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences:

  • Selected Theme: Minimal & Elegant, Midnight Ember, Cosmic Nebula, or Pastel Lavender.
  • Selected Web Scene Mode: Corner Web, Center Orb, or Create Your Own Web.
  • Animation & Physics Settings: Breeze intensity, silk elasticity, orange dew drops, floating leaf particles, and dot grid visibility.

These settings never leave your device and are permanently deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall WebArt.

4. Permissions Requested

  • Live Wallpaper Service (android.software.live_wallpaper / BIND_WALLPAPER): Used exclusively so you can set the interactive spider web canvas as your Android Home Screen and Lock Screen Live Wallpaper.

WebArt does not request Internet, Camera, Microphone, Location, or Storage permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

Because WebArt does not collect any data from any user, it is safe for audiences of all ages and fully complies with COPPA and GDPR requirements.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

BallMaze3D: Sky Adventure

Official Privacy Policy
Developer: Suraj Sahani (martialcoder) Effective Date: November 2, 2026 Package: com.martialcoder.ballmaze3d Zero Personal Data Collected

1. Introduction

Welcome to BallMaze3D: Sky Adventure, a 3D floating sky road and marble physics adventure game designed for Android devices, developed by Suraj Sahani (MartialCoder) ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, usage, and protection when you download, install, and play BallMaze3D from the Google Play Store.

Core Privacy Principle: BallMaze3D is built under a strict privacy-by-design philosophy. We do NOT collect, store, share, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), user accounts, contacts, location data, photos, or browsing history to any external servers.

2. Information Collection and Device Hardware Usage

BallMaze3D operates completely offline on your local device. The game interacts only with standard device hardware strictly to provide real-time 3D marble physics controls and feedback:

🎯 Motion & Tilt Controls (Accelerometer)

When using Hybrid (Touch + Tilt) or Tilt control modes, device accelerometer readings are processed ephemerally in local memory strictly to steer the marble along the floating sky road. Sensor readings are never recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.

📳 Haptic Feedback & Vibration

The game uses on-device vibration hardware to provide tactile feedback when passing through checkpoint rings, collecting golden stars, hitting boost pads, or colliding with obstacles.

💾 Local Sandbox Storage

Gameplay state—such as stage progress, collected stars, unlocked marble skins, and control mode preferences—is kept strictly within the Android application sandbox on your device and never leaves your device.

🔊 Audio Playback

All sound effects (rolling chimes, star pickups, checkpoint fanfare, and obstacle audio) are synthesized and played locally on your device. BallMaze3D never requests or accesses your microphone.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

BallMaze3D does not integrate third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or user profiling analytics.

Because the game is distributed via the Google Play Store, Google Play may provide standard, aggregated, and anonymized diagnostic metrics (such as crash logs or ANR reports) via Google Play Console solely to help maintain stability across Android devices.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

BallMaze3D is a family-friendly 3D marble adventure suitable for players of all ages. Because the application does not collect, request, or store any personal data, identifiers, or communication channels from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

5. Data Security and Offline Operation

All runtime preferences and gameplay state remain isolated inside Android's secure application sandbox. BallMaze3D is playable 100% offline with no internet connection required.

6. User Control & Data Deletion Rights

Because no personal data is transmitted or stored on external servers, you have full control over all local app data on your device:

  • Resetting Game Data: Clear local data anytime via Settings → Apps → BallMaze3D → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Complete Removal: Uninstalling BallMaze3D removes all application files and local data immediately.

Introduction

Welcome to Match the Following ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed and published by Suraj Sahani (martialcoder).

We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of everyone who plays our game. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data privacy, explaining that the App operates completely offline without collecting or tracking your personal information.

2

Information Collection & Use

Match the Following does NOT collect, harvest, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, register, or provide an email, name, or phone number to play.
  • No Location Tracking: The App never accesses GPS or device location.
  • No Biometric or Media Access: The App does not access your photos, files, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • No Advertising Trackers: The App does not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers or analytics SDKs that profile users.
Privacy By Design: All game mechanics, line drawing calculations, and sound effects are processed locally and offline on your device processor.
3

Local Device Storage

The App may save basic non-identifiable gameplay progress (such as completed levels, star ratings, and sound mute preferences) locally using standard Android private internal storage.

This data remains strictly stored inside the application sandbox on your physical device. It is never uploaded to any cloud database or third-party server.

4

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our application is designed as an educational, family-friendly puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever:

  • We do not solicit or gather data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • Parents and guardians can be confident that their children can play safely without privacy risks or data harvesting.
5

Device Permissions

Match the Following requests only minimal, non-sensitive standard system permissions required for core interactive feedback:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE – Used solely to provide subtle tactile haptic vibration when a match is made.

No dangerous or privacy-sensitive runtime permissions are requested.

6

Third-Party Services

The App is distributed via official app marketplaces (such as the Google Play Store). These platform providers may collect anonymous, aggregated diagnostic crash data or distribution telemetry according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
7

Data Deletion & User Rights

Because all game state is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • Clearing the App's data or cache via Android Settings permanently wipes all local game progress.
  • Uninstalling the App automatically and completely deletes all associated local data from your device.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage users to periodically review this page.

1. Target Audience & Child Safety

Primary Play: Color & Learn is dedicated to providing a joyful, creative, and 100% safe coloring environment designed specifically for toddlers and young children (ages 3–8).

  • No account creation, sign-ins, or passwords are ever required.
  • No personal information (names, emails, phone numbers) is requested or stored.
  • No social media integrations, chat functions, or public interactions.
  • Strict adherence to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
lock

2. Information Collection & Usage

We collect ZERO personal data.

  • 100% Offline Gameplay: All interactive drawing and coloring activities execute completely locally on your device.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not track user behavior, usage habits, or device telemetry.
  • No Location Tracking: The application never accesses GPS or IP-based location data.

🛡️ Guarantee: Your child's creativity remains strictly private and stays on your personal device at all times.

toggle_on

3. Device Permissions

The application only requests minimal system access required for tactile play:

  • Haptic Vibration: Used strictly to produce subtle tactile button press feedback during coloring.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Storage, or SMS.
do_not_disturb

4. No Advertisements & No In-App Purchases

To ensure an uninterrupted, stress-free creative space for kids:

  • Zero Advertisements: There are no banner ads, video ads, or interstitial ads.
  • Zero In-App Purchases: No microtransactions, subscriptions, or hidden fees.
  • No External Web Links: The app contains no links to third-party websites or external stores.

5. Regulatory Compliance

COPPA Compliance: We do not collect or distribute any personal information from children under 13.

Google Play Families Policy: Our app is certified compliant with all Google Play family safety and age-appropriate guidelines.

GDPR Compliance: European Union privacy and data minimization standards are fully observed.

Privacy Policy

OneLess — Minimalist Focus, Digital Wellbeing & Productivity

📦 Package: com.martialcoder.oneless 📅 Last Updated: September 5, 2026 🛡️ GDPR / CCPA / COPPA Compliant

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to OneLess ("the App"), developed by MartialCoder (Suraj Sahani) ("we", "us", or "our"). OneLess is designed as a minimalist, distraction-free digital wellbeing and productivity application created to help you declutter your digital life, focus on what matters, and cultivate intentional device usage.

We believe that your digital privacy is a fundamental human right. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding data collection, storage, device permissions, and security when you use the OneLess Android mobile application.

🔒 Local-First & Zero Harvesting Pledge: OneLess is built with a strict local-first architecture. Your focus goals, digital detox logs, habits, preferences, and customized configurations are stored exclusively on your device. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your private data.

2. Information Collection and Handling

OneLess operates with extreme data minimization principles. We only process data strictly necessary to provide core digital wellbeing functionality:

  • Local User Data & Preferences: Your customized focus modes, task lists, habits, minimalist launcher shortcuts, usage timers, and app settings are stored locally on your device storage (via local database / SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.
  • Anonymous Diagnostic & Performance Telemetry: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as crash stack traces, Android OS version, and device hardware model) may be captured via standard Google Play Services solely to troubleshoot bugs, prevent crashes, and maintain app stability.
  • No Mandatory Account Registration: OneLess does not require account creation, email sign-ups, or social media logins to access its primary features.

3. Permissions Used by OneLess

To enable seamless focus tracking, minimalist notifications, and habit reminders, OneLess may request certain Android system permissions:

Permission Purpose & Necessity Status
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Allows OneLess to send subtle focus reminders, habit nudges, or digital detox completion alerts. Optional / User Granted
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Ensures high-precision focus session timers and scheduled quiet intervals fire accurately even during device Doze mode. Functional
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS / QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Used strictly on-device if you enable intentional app launching or screen time awareness to display and minimize distracting apps. No usage history is ever transmitted off-device. Optional (On Demand)
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used exclusively for checking app updates, verifying Google Play Integrity, and sending non-identifiable crash diagnostic reports. Standard

You can grant, review, or revoke any optional permission at any time through your Android device's Settings → Apps → OneLess → Permissions.

4. Data Storage and Sandbox Security

We employ modern Android security best practices to protect your data:

  • On-Device Sandbox Isolation: All user configurations are stored within private application sandboxes that cannot be accessed by other applications installed on your device.
  • Encrypted Connections: Any outbound network interactions (such as version updates or store services) use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Zero Data Brokerage: We do not share, sell, trade, or monetize user data with third-party advertisers, trackers, or marketing brokers.

5. Third-Party Services

OneLess may integrate standard, trustworthy platform services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Integrity: Used for secure app distribution, license verification, and in-app updates. Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

OneLess complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child data protection standards. Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a minor has provided information to us, please reach out to us and we will promptly ensure it is addressed.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Regardless of your region (including the European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you maintain full autonomy over your data:

  • Right to Access & Control: All your digital focus data is readily visible and accessible inside the app at all times.
  • Right to Complete Erasure: You can permanently wipe all data and preferences at any moment by clearing the app's cache and storage in Android Settings, or simply by uninstalling the application.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against users for exercising their privacy rights.
